# The cold kana rows in row space, and their shared direction rescaled (2026-10-03)

Two reads on the rows the reseed arms trained, against the seeds'. The user
asked (10-03) how the reseed rows' cos sim differs from the seed's, whether
a per-row gradient schedule explains it, what kind of embedding the rows'
geometry is, and then how the render changes when the rows' shared direction
(the "stick" of a burr on a stick) is shortened.

**Verdict.**
- **Cold training lands at the same distance from the 0921 seed whatever the
  data.** Same-row cos 0.43–0.45 for `retrain_kana` (= the 0930 seed's kana
  rows) and both reseed arms; the reseed arms sit at 0.58–0.59 to
  `retrain_kana` and 0.66 to each other (mean directions 0.99).
- **The reseed data took out most of the exposure skew.** Per-glyph loss
  share spans 4× in the reseed arms against 22× in `retrain_kana`, and the
  glyphs' mean σ 0.45–0.51 against 0.50–0.80. In `retrain_kana` exposure
  sets a row's norm (ρ 0.83) and its share of the shared direction (ρ 0.65);
  in the reseed arms both are weak. Exposure does not explain the cos to the
  seed (ρ −0.15).
- **The rows are a burr on a stick, off the T5 manifold.** A shared mean
  holds 29–34 % of the delta energy (stick |m| 138–148), and the per-row
  residuals ("spikes", 206–216) are near-orthogonal and near-even in length.
  The effective rows are 1.37× the T5 table's mean norm, and their nearest
  T5 row is at cos 0.30 (T5 to T5: 0.68). The stick is not along T5's main
  directions, and the spikes are T5-random.
- **The 0921 seed's long spikes are the glyphs no cold arm goes back to.**
  Its 15 longest residuals (か ニ だ ノ ツ で …) read cos 0.19–0.25 to the
  cold rows, the rest 0.45–0.47.
- **Shortening the stick costs identity and keeps the repeats.** 0930 seed
  on `sent`: ≤ 1 edit 45 → 25 → 11 → 2 → 0 of 92 at s = 1 / 0.75 / 0.5 /
  0.25 / 0, dup 40 → 50–53. The region and slot count stay down to s = 0.5;
  at 0 the base's garble comes back.
- **On `reseed_anchor`, ±10 % moves nothing.** s = 0.9: ≤ 1 edit 13 vs 18
  of 64 (0 / 5, p 0.06); s = 1.1: 15 vs 18 (p 0.45), dup 46 vs 40 (p 0.18).
  The arm sits far under the 0930 floor on the same keys (≤ 1 edit 18 vs
  34, p 0.004), and its stick is only 7 % shorter than the seed's (137 vs
  147). The reseed's deficit is in the spikes, not the stick.

## 2. Exposure and σ per row

The trainer gives every row one AdamW and one lr schedule, and the
`Batcher` reshuffles every epoch, so no row's draws cluster in time. What
differs per row is the data: how many items carry the glyph, its share of
their loss (Σ 1 / glyphs), and the items' bands.

| | items per glyph | loss share | mean σ band mid |
|---|---|---|---|
| `retrain_kana` | 82–1 335 | 14–303 (22×) | 0.50–0.80 |
| `recap hp` | 205–934 | 52–208 (4×) | 0.45–0.51 |
| `anchor` | 197–955 | 47–206 (4×) | 0.45–0.51 |

Spearman over the 162 rows:

| | `retrain_kana` | `recap hp` | `anchor` |
|---|---|---|---|
| share · cos to 0921 | −0.16 | −0.15 | −0.16 |
| σ · cos to 0921 | +0.28 | +0.34 | +0.38 |
| share · row norm | **+0.83** | +0.39 | +0.37 |
| σ · row norm | −0.73 | −0.11 | −0.09 |
| share · energy along the arm's mean | **+0.65** | +0.24 | +0.15 |
| σ · energy along the arm's mean | −0.61 | −0.07 | +0.10 |
| share · cos to `retrain_kana` | — | +0.51 | +0.49 |
| σ · cos to `retrain_kana` | — | −0.54 | −0.46 |

- In `retrain_kana` the frequent glyphs sit in the low-σ word items and the
  rare ones in the high-σ lone items, so share and σ cannot be separated
  there. The shared direction grows with share; it is not the high-σ
  gradient's doing.
- Where the reseed arms agree with `retrain_kana`, it is on the glyphs both
  drew often.
- The arms with the flat exposure read under `retrain_kana` (plain words
  official 15 / 11 vs 33 of 104), so flattening exposure did not buy reads.

## 3. What kind of embedding (`geometry.py`)

| | norm | mean energy | nearest-T5 cos (median) |
|---|---|---|---|
| T5 rows (`llm_adapter.embed`) | 212 ± 35 | 0.24 (162 random) | 0.68 to another T5 row |
| pack rows | 187 ± 17 | 0.28 | 0.37 |
| 0921 seed, effective | 307 ± 27 | 0.32 | 0.27 |
| `retrain_kana`, effective | 287 ± 15 | 0.40 | 0.32 |
| `recap hp`, effective | 291 ± 17 | 0.36 | 0.30 |

- Off the T5 manifold, and moved further off by training (pack 0.37 →
  0.30): the textual-inversion / soft-prompt pattern, not a word-embedding
  one. They are closer to each other (nearest cos 0.53) than to any T5 row.
- The stick is not T5's: 6–9 % of the mean delta's energy on T5's top-10
  principal directions, cos 0.22–0.25 to the T5 mean. The centered rows put
  6.5–6.8 % of their energy in T5's top-50 span (chance 4.9 %).
- 1.37× the T5 norm is about 2 σ of T5's spread, far from the 20× norm
  inflation that norm-fixing inversion methods start from. The norm
  levers here were neutral (`p1_cap`, P0, `delta_scale`).

## 4. The burr: stick and spikes

| delta | stick \|m\| | spike \|row − m\| | CV | min – max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0921 seed | 144 | 227 ± 30 | **0.134** | 184 – 362 |
| `retrain_kana` | 148 | 206 ± 18 | 0.085 | 172 – 302 |
| `recap hp` | 141 | 216 ± 21 | 0.096 | 176 – 323 |
| `anchor` | 138 | 213 ± 23 | 0.110 | 170 – 349 |

Effective (pack + delta): T5 stick 104 / spike 185 (spike : stick 1.78),
pack 99 / 159 (1.61), `recap hp` 175 / 233 (1.33), `retrain_kana` 181 / 223
(1.23). Every embedding table here is a burr on a stick; training lengthens
the stick against the spikes, most where exposure is most skewed. The
spikes are longer than the stick (each row 50–60° off its axis). Centered,
the mean pairwise cos is −1/(n − 1) by construction; the spread (|cos| p95
0.15) and the PR ≈ 70–80 are the information.

The spike lengths rank alike in the two reseed arms (ρ 0.67), less with
`retrain_kana` (0.5) and not with the 0921 seed (0.1–0.2). The 0921 seed's
15 longest spikes — か ニ だ ノ ツ で あ ェ ォ づ ャ ぢ ぐ せ ぴ — are where
the cold rows leave it (cos 0.19 / 0.23 / 0.25 on those vs 0.45–0.47 on the
rest; ρ spike length · cos −0.38 / −0.31 / −0.22).

## What this says about levers

- **The stick as an inference knob is closed both ways.** Shorter costs
  identity at about `delta_scale`'s rate; ±10 % on the reseed rows is inside
  the noise.
- **The stick as a training prior is `--pin_dir`** (2026-09-16,
  `../../finished/cjk_renderable_anima/reports/transplant_2026_09_16.md`:
  frozen family direction, residual trained ⟂ it — `swap` 20 vs 9 at 500
  steps, 32 vs 46 at 2 k). The cold runs rebuild the same stick (cos
  0.89–0.99, |m| 138–148), so a prior would hand them what they find anyway.
  The only untried part would be a fixed spike radius; norms have been
  neutral throughout.
- **What separates `retrain_kana` from the reseed arms is in the spikes** —
  the per-glyph identity the data teaches — not in the stick or in exposure.
