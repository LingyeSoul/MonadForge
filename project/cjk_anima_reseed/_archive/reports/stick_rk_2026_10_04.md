# stick_rk: retrain_kana's stick re-fit on the scene tiers (2026-10-04)

From the user (10-04, after `stick_scene_2026_10_04.md`): does a stick-only
re-fit move `retrain_kana`, the arm that reads best, on `stick_nolonegrid`'s
data; then (same day) the same with every item over the full σ range.

**Verdict.**
- **On retrain_kana's rows the stick carries the word fit, and the re-fit
  loses it.** `stick_rk_nolonegrid` moves the stick about as far as
  `stick_nolonegrid` moved kana_up's (cos 0.922 to its start) and words fall
  to kana_up's level: official 19 vs 33 (4 / 18, p 0.004), ≤ 1 edit 58 vs 73
  (7 / 22, p 0.008), ≤ 2 edits 80 vs 94 (4 / 18, p 0.004), dup 67 vs 52;
  singles tie (official 44 vs 47, p 0.63). 11 of 13 words lose.
- **The move is not toward kana_up's stick.** cos with kana_up − retrain_kana
  0.11 (full band 0.18), and both re-fits end further from kana_up's stick
  (0.931 → 0.876 / 0.865). The same data moves both rows' sticks alike (move
  cos 0.47 with `stick_nolonegrid`'s): render-neutral on kana_up's spikes,
  the word fit on retrain_kana's. `stick_2026_10_03.md`'s "the deficit is in
  the spikes, not the stick" holds for which rows read, not for how the
  read reacts to the stick.
- **Full band buys scene and pays the singles.** `stick_rk_full_band` (σ
  0–0.99): words official 20 (8 / 21, p 0.024), ≤ 1 / ≤ 2 edits no longer
  apart (p 0.14 / 0.24), singles official 34 (5 / 18, p 0.011; ひ 7 → 2,
  と 5 → 2). EN-ref cos out up on every group against retrain_kana (words
  +0.007, singles +0.009, p01 +0.024; p 3e-4 / 5e-6 / 9e-4), flat white down
  (words 0.114 → 0.070, p01 0.671 → 0.613). The pictures stay retrain_kana's
  — a step toward the EN ref, not `stick_nlg_high`'s change of layout.
- **No stick-only re-fit here is a strict gain on retrain_kana.**

## What it leaves open

- **Why retrain_kana's spikes need their own stick and kana_up's do not**:
  a move of ~60 off the kana_up axis costs one and not the other.
- **Full band's scene gain without the singles cost**: whether the scene
  step and the singles loss are one move, as kana_up's gain and scene cost
  were (`probe_split_2026_10_04.md`).
