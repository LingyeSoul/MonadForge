# ball_rk: the ball trained cold on retrain_kana's stick (2026-10-04)

From the user (10-04, after `ball_2026_10_04.md`): the grid ball read at
retrain_kana's level once moved onto retrain_kana's stick (`gs_rkstick`) —
does a ball trained *on* that stick do as well? Two arms, one variable each:
`ball_rk` (`grid_small r0`'s data, the stick held instead of free), then
`ball_rk_bubble` (the same with the reseed table's bubble tiers as data).

**Verdict.**
- **A ball trains on a held stick, and the stick changes what it learns.**
  On grid_small r0's data, `ball_rk` keeps the scene the transplant lost
  (words EN-ref cos 0.936 vs `gs_rkstick` 0.920, +0.015, p 0.054; the p01
  flat pink canvas and near-blank page are gone) and reads fewer words:
  official 8 vs 16 (1 / 9, p 0.021), ≤ 1 edit 19 vs 28 (2 / 11, p 0.022).
  Against `rk_self` it ties on official (3 / 6, p 0.51), loses ≤ 2 edits
  (0 / 8, p 0.008). The grid ball trained on its own shorter stick (134)
  carried the grid's picture; trained on retrain_kana's, it does not.
- **Bubble data: glyphs and scene at retrain_kana's level, exact words
  fall.** `ball_rk_bubble` singles official 17 = `rk_self`'s 17 (3 / 3),
  repeats 0 (the lowest of every arm), scene tied (words −0.007, p 0.09;
  singles −0.0002). Words official 2 vs 11 (1 / 10, p 0.012), vs `ball_rk`
  0 / 6 (p 0.031); near misses tie (≤ 1 edit 23 vs 25, ≤ 2 edits 32 vs 35,
  p 0.75 / 0.38). On the p00 sheet the words sit in retrain_kana's top banner
  one glyph off: `かさんがえ`, `ここうえんん`, `たすけはて`, `なにししてる`.
  The glyphs are right; the word does not fill the banner's slots (dup 23
  vs 17).
- **The read / scene trade moved to the ball and did not go away.** The
  grid ball moved onto the stick bought words with scene; trained on the
  stick, either data keeps the scene and pays exact words. No ball trained
  on retrain_kana's stick beats retrain_kana's own.
- The bubble ball is the shortest (183 against 201–209) and the closest to
  retrain_kana's (cos 0.512). Whether its length is why the words miss is
  not read.

## 1. The runs

`cjk_scale.train(ball_on=…)` (new): every trained row starts at
retrain_kana's mean over the run's rows (|156.0| in the run's row units),
and after every optimizer step each row is shifted by the rows' mean drift,
so the mean stays at that value and only the rows less it train (a gradient
hook is not enough: AdamW's per-element scaling un-centres a centred
gradient). The stick held to cos 1.0, |156.05| at every logged step.
Context (the frozen rows, the merge base) is retrain_kana's merged rows, so
`trained.pt` is retrain_kana's table with the 81 hiragana rows replaced —
the shape `probe_split`'s swap arms read. 81 hiragana rows, 135 steps / row
= 10 935, the trainer of record otherwise (lr 1e-3 cosine, μ 0, free pull
1e-3).
