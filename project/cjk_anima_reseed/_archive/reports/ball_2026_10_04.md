  `gs_rkstick` (grid_small r0's hiragana rows less their mean plus

  its own stick (`gs_self`): words official 13 / 2 (p 0.007), dup 0 / 15

  in EN-ref cos (en_cos −0.018, 10 / 26, p 0.006; outside the box −0.016,
  p 0.03); on p01 it writes the word large and centred on a flat pink canvas
  where retrain_kana writes a top banner over the bed. `gs_self` keeps the
  scene (singles en_cos +0.025, p 0.10) and repeats. Singles tie retrain_kana
  on EN-ref cos. `stick_scene_2026_10_04.md`'s "the look is in the spikes"
  now has its grid case: the flat-canvas look reads out once the stick lets
  the ball read.
- **The user's read of the sheets** (10-04, `ball_sheets`): `gs_rkstick`
  renders the glyphs best of the three, but fits the text into the scene
  less well than retrain_kana.
- **The swap is symmetric: the words read on the stick** (§ 4).
  retrain_kana's ball on the grid stick (`rk_gsstick`) falls to kana_up's

- **A weak ball reads on no stick.** `recap_h0`'s ball (lower edge 0, cos
  0.24) reads 0 words on its own stick and on retrain_kana's (official 0 /
