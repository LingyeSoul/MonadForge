# kana_mix read, the large tier's band off the gradient, kana_big (2026-10-03)

Three steps, from the user (10-03): read `kana_mix` (the table's first trained
arm); ask why the reseed arms stay under `retrain_kana` — glyph size or the σ
band; read the band of a tier above `grid_44` off the gradient instead of
taking `retrain_kana`'s 0.7–0.9 (that tier draws the glyph alone on a blank
page at eval); train it in as `kana_big`.

**Verdict.**
- **`kana_mix` ties the reseed arms and stays far under `retrain_kana`.**
  Plain read: words official 11 / 104 (anchor 11, recap hp 15, retrain_kana
  33), ≤ 1 edit 34 (40 / 42 / 73); singles official 31 / 112 (21 / 30 / 47).
  Paired vs `reseed_anchor`, singles official 20 / 10 (p 0.10; hiragana

  has the lowest singles cos (0.928) and box IoU (0.14) and reads best.
- **What `kana_mix` lacks against `retrain_kana` is a large glyph trained
  high.** By glyph instance: ≥ 64 px 0.8 % vs 19.7 %; ≥ 40 px with a band
  reaching 0.85, 0.0 % vs 28.3 %. The ≥ 40 px share alone (29 vs 34 %) hid it.
- **The band moves up with the glyph.** Upper edge (f's half point) 0.74 /
  0.82 / 0.88 at 44 / 65 / 97 px in a grid; lower edge (‖I‖ at half its
  peak) 0.51 / 0.63 / 0.70. `grid_44` reproduces the 10-02 read (0.75).
  0.7–0.9 is the gradient's band for a ≈ 95 px glyph — but `retrain_kana`'s
  `b0709` drew 40–383 px in it, so its ≤ 65 px glyphs trained up to 0.08
  above their edge, and at σ 0.9 even a 97 px glyph's gradient is 83 %
  layout (f 0.17).
- **`grid_64` (65 px, σ 0.65–0.8) is the tier.** Its ‖I‖ peak (57) is the
  largest of the five tiers read and its band stays under 0.85.
  `kana_big` = `kana_mix` + `grid_64` at 12.5 % of the items.
- **`kana_big` ties `kana_mix`.** Words official 9 vs 11 / 104 (7 / 9,
  p 0.8), ≤ 1 edit 35 vs 34; singles official 27 vs 31 / 112 (10 / 14,
  p 0.54). The one paired count under 0.05 goes the other way: words ≤ 2
  edits 62 vs 76 (15 / 29, p 0.049). Under `retrain_kana` as before (words
  official 4 / 28, ≤ 1 edit 5 / 43). 12.5 % of the items at 65 px and
  σ 0.65–0.8 is not the gap (§ 4).
