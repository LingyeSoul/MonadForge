# stick_fit: kana_up's stick re-fit with its spikes held (2026-10-04)

From the user (10-04, after `probe_split_2026_10_04.md` § 4: retrain_kana's
stick on kana_up's spikes reads retrain_kana's words and brings its scene
cost): train the stick and the ball apart. First, the stick alone: hold
kana_up's spikes, train their shared mean on kana_up's data with and without
the lone tiers. The question: is the scene cost the lone tiers' contribution
to the stick, and does the rest of the data pick a stick with the word fit?
The same day, two questions on the side: is the retrain's kanji family the
same burr, and can a data mix alone train the ball only?

**Verdict.**
- **The stick re-fit moves the stick and nothing on the render.** Both
  runs move the stick by 45 (|m| 141 → 152–153, cos 0.95 to kana_up's),
  as far as retrain_kana's stick sits from kana_up's (54). Words, singles and
  scene tie kana_up: words ≤ 1 edit 51 / 54 vs 55 of 104, singles official
  35 / 40 vs 39 of 112 (every pair p ≥ 0.2); singles p01 cos out 0.779 /
  0.774 vs 0.769, flat white 0.470 / 0.475 vs 0.501. On the sheets they draw
  kana_up's pictures; only glyphs change inside the same slots.
- **The stick has render-neutral directions, and the reseed data does not
  pull toward retrain_kana's.** The two runs' moves share cos 0.40 and are
  orthogonal to retrain_kana − kana_up (cos 0.06 / 0.02); the two re-fit
  sticks sit 50 apart and render alike. Distance between sticks does not
  measure an effect; the word fit `up_rkstick` showed is along one
  direction, and kana_up's data, with or without lone, does not find it.
- **The lone tiers' look is not in the stick.** The user's read of the
  sheets: the lone training crams the Japanese into an area or draws a
  white canvas. On the p01 singles the white canvas (く り う も) and the
  pasted panel (ひ, a white column on the duvet; あ と on a white patch)
  are kana_up's and stay in both re-fits, `stick_nolone` included. Ordered by
  high-σ lone training (retrain_kana > kana_up > kana_mix), singles flat white
  goes 0.236 > 0.196 > 0.154. If the lone tiers make the look, it is in the
  spikes. (`stick_nolone` kept the grids, flat canvases too; with every flat
  tier out the look stays — `stick_scene_2026_10_04.md`.)
- **The retrain's kanji family is the same burr** (§ 4). Four disjoint cold
  batches build one stick (cos 0.96–0.99, |m| 139–148, 35 % of the
  energy); kana and kanji sticks are at cos 0.75–0.80; the 0921 seed's
  kanji stick at 0.65–0.74 to the retrain's. The stick is the data recipe's,
  not the glyphs'.
- **No data mix trains the ball alone** (§ 5). Cold low-band flat grids
  (`grid_small r0`) build a near-full stick in kana_up's direction (134,
  cos 0.92); σ from 0 (`recap_h0`) lowers its share 0.215 → 0.181 and reads
  worse; high σ alone (`b7593`) builds a stick at cos 0.47–0.50 and reads 0.
  Warm, the test is `stick_full`: on its own recipe the stick still moves
  (cos 0.956), so kana_up's stick is not the fixed point of its data with
  its spikes held. A ball-only run needs the mean held by the optimizer.

## 1. The runs

`cjk_scale.train(stick_only=True)`: rows warm from `kana_up/trained.pt`
(also the context), every one of the 166 trained rows takes the sum of the
166 rows' gradients (a hook on `delta.raw`), so AdamW moves them all by one
vector; the rows less their mean stay kana_up's (max drift 3e-4). Everything
else is the trainer of record (lr 1e-3 cosine, warmup 0.1, μ 0, free pull
1e-3, batch 4); a fresh AdamW at lr 1e-3 on rows trained to lr 0.

## 5. Can a data mix train the ball alone

The user (10-04): would a grid-only or low-σ mix train the spikes and leave
the stick? Two answers.

**Cold, no.** The hiragana sticks (81 rows) of the cold band arms of
`grid_small_lone_2026_10_02.md`:

| arm | bands | \|m\| | spike | mean energy | stick cos retrain_kana / kana_up | row cos retrain_kana |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| retrain_kana | | 156 | 209 | 0.36 | 1 / 0.93 | 1 |
| kana_up | | 149 | 212 | 0.33 | 0.93 / 1 | 0.64 |
| `grid_small r0` | per px 0.3–0.5, 0.5–0.7 | 134 | 199 | 0.31 | 0.88 / 0.92 | 0.60 |
| `grid_small b7593` | 0.75–0.93 | 127 | 207 | 0.27 | 0.47 / 0.50 | 0.22 |
| `grid_lone r0` | per px | 94 | 170 | 0.23 | 0.82 / 0.87 | 0.52 |
| `grid_lone recap_hp` | half peak – half point | 91 | 174 | 0.22 | 0.76 / 0.87 | 0.44 |
| `grid_lone recap_h0` | 0 – half point | 78 | 165 | 0.18 | 0.65 / 0.77 | 0.34 |

(`grid_lone` trained 60 steps / row against `grid_small`'s 135; compare
lengths within a family.) Low-band flat grids build the stick in the scene
runs' direction. The lower edge at 0 lowers its share and turns it, but
reads worse; high σ alone builds another stick and reads nothing. By
`grad_identity_2026_10_02.md` § 2 two rows' gradients on one item share
cos ≈ 0.15 at every σ, so every draw feeds the stick; grids have the
lowest glyph-dependent share of any tier (f 0.3–0.5, `grad_bands`),
`bubble1` at σ ≤ 0.6 the highest (0.8–0.9).

**Warm, not on this recipe.** The stick's gradient vanishes in expectation
only where the data's layout is what the current stick already renders:
the stick is its recipe's fixed point. `stick_full` is that test on kana_up,
and it moved (§ 3). (A part of the move may be the fresh optimizer at lr
1e-3 on a run that ended at lr 0; the plateau at lr 1e-3, rather than a
walk, reads as a pull.) To train the spikes alone, hold the mean in the
optimizer: after every step subtract the rows' mean drift from every row
(a gradient hook is not enough — AdamW's per-element scaling un-centres
centred gradients). Unlike `--pin_dir` (09-16: each row ⟂ m̂, the row denied
the stick component the EN frame wants), this holds only the mean; a row
keeps its own coefficient along it.

## What it leaves open

- **The lone tiers in the spikes**: kana_up's rows, the mean held, the
  spikes trained on kana_up's data less `lone_*` (the `stick_nolone` mirror;
  less `grid_*` too after `stick_scene_2026_10_04.md`), read for the white
  canvas and the pasted panel on p01 singles.
- **The direction `up_rkstick` moved along**: kana_up's spikes on sticks
  part way from kana_up's to retrain_kana's (no training) — do the word fit
  and the scene cost move together along it.
- **Stick-only on retrain_kana's data** from kana_up's spikes: whether that
  data pulls the stick toward retrain_kana's (the fixed-point reading
  directly).
