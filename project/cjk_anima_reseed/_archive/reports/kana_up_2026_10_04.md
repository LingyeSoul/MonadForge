# kana_up: every tier's upper σ edge + 0.1 (2026-10-04)

From the user (10-04): the table's upper edges sit at the gradient's half
point (`grid_44` 0.75, read 0.74 on 10-03) and `retrain_kana` trained above
them; `kana_big` (a 65 px tier inside its band) tied `kana_mix`
(`grid_64_2026_10_03.md` § 4). So `kana_up` = `kana_mix` with every tier's
upper edge moved up 0.1, capped at 0.9 (`upper_shift` in the run config);
shares, lower edges, rows, seed and steps unchanged.

**Verdict.**
- **The text reads better: the first data recipe of the reseed that moves the
  read.** Words ≤ 1 edit 55 vs 34 / 104 (paired 32 / 11, p 0.002), ≤ 2 edits
  88 vs 76 (19 / 7, p 0.029), le1c 88 vs 67 (25 / 4, p 1e-4). Exact matches
  move less: words official 14 vs 11 (7 / 4, p 0.55), singles official 39 vs
  31 / 112 (15 / 7, p 0.13).
- **The gain is hiragana.** Hiragana words ≤ 1 edit 26 / 7 (p 0.001), le1c
  20 / 2; katakana words flat (≤ 1 edit 6 / 4), and against `retrain_kana`
  katakana words ≤ 1 edit 0 / 10.
- **Against `retrain_kana`, the near-miss gap closes; exact matches do not.**
  Words ≤ 2 edits 7 / 13 (p 0.26), singles official 7 / 15 (p 0.13;
  `kana_big` 9 / 29); words official still 9 / 28 (p 0.003), ≤ 1 edit
  13 / 31. Repeats and dup stay `kana_up`'s (singles repeats 16 / 2, words
  dup 31 / 10).
- **The scene degrades (user's read of the sheets), the same way
  `retrain_kana`'s does.** EN-reference cos outside the text box: words
  0.949 (`kana_mix` 0.947), singles 0.934 (0.942), `retrain_kana` 0.927; on
  the bare-bed prompt p01 singles 0.860 / 0.882 / 0.840. On the sheets the
  text takes the plain canvas over: a large block across the duvet with the
  girl pushed to a corner (たいせつ p01), smeared colour (パソコン p01).
