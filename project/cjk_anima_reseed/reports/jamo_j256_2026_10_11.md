(~ = the whole-image read is one syllable sharing a jamo with the target; one
glyph / 4+ = whole-image reads of one letter / of four or more, counted on
`reads.md`.)

- **The first step that moves precision, not recall.** J64 → J128 moved jamo R
  (0.46 → 0.56) with jamo P flat at 0.20. J128 → J256 holds jamo R (0.56 →
  0.55) and lifts jamo P 0.20 → 0.27 and glyph P 0.05 → 0.14: less else drawn
  beside the target, more of it the target.
- Exact 3 → 5: 시 인 라 아 래. J128's 성 and 소 are lost (잠, 낸수…); 시 is
  the only hit every J arm from J96 on shares.
- J256 draws a lone glyph on 11 / 32 (J128 5), text of four letters or more on
  8 (J128 13).
- Two variables changed together: the set (256 rows) and the total steps
  (double J128's). J64-112 (J64 at J128's total) fell below J64, so steps on a
  fixed set do not explain the gain by themselves; a J128 at 14 336 steps
  would separate them.
