# xattn_box — a spatial prior on the string's cross-attn keys (2026-10-15)

SpaG-DiT's CDPE (text tokens given image coordinates before attention;
paper unreleased, read from its abstract only) carried over to Anima's
cross-attn: the text keys carry no image position, so the prior is an
additive logit bias `(N_q, L_ctx)` (`_ctx_k_bias`, now 2-D), cond forward
only, σ ≥ 0.85, β = 4, no row trained. Target box = the EN reference's
box for the same line (the base's own place for it; 64 / 96 ruler strings
have one). Rows `seed_1008` (jp_v1) on the punct pack; control = its cached
ruler renders, paired on the 64. Probe: `probes/xattn_box.py`.

Results (gitignored): `results/20261015-2150-ruler-sensitive-xattn_box/`
(xb4, xs4), `results/20261015-2202-ruler-sensitive-xattn_tags/` (xt4).

## Arms

- `xb4` — every string token (first to last ext id) −β outside the box.
- `xs4` — glyph k −β outside its own tategaki cell in the box.
- `xt4` — `xb4`, and the lowercase `speech bubble` / `japanese text` tag
  tokens −β outside the box (the clause's `Japanese text` left alone).

## What it says

- The off-box mass of the string's keys was keeping generic text out of
  the rest of the page; suppressed alone, it goes to the bubble / text tags
  and they letter the page. A region's text is drawn by the string **and**
  the tags together — the same coupling `probe_jl` (no row direction moves
  the box without the page) and `probe_twin` (credit spreads to the
  neighbours) met from the row side.
- A box prior moves *where* a little and not *how many*: slot count and
  repetition do not follow placement. Closed as an inference lever; a
  training-side region credit would have to carry the tags with it.

Caveats: the hooks-idle check re-rendered r47 at mean |Δpx| 8.2 against the
cached render (session noise median 3.8, max 18.9); r52 / r65 hold other
ext ids in their tags, so their span ran wide.
