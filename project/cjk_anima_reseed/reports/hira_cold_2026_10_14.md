## Verdict

1. **Both arms lose to the floor.** On words they score 10 / 26 against 19 /
   26, and on singles 6 and 11 against 18.
2. **The identity is mostly there. The failure is repetition.** Singles
   *contained* 17 – 18 against the floor's 19, but *dup* is 12 – 17 against 1:
   あ is drawn ああああ, を as をを, and the words double glyphs (かかなししい,
   かかんががえ, たたいつつ). The grid-heavy mix (≈ 6 glyphs an item) together with
   ¼ of `retrain_kana`'s in-word exposure gives a row that fills cells. This is
   § 8's risk ("words and `dup` decide, not singles"), read as stated. Lone
   glyphs also pick up Latin filler (`xxx.com laone`) more often than on the
   floor.
3. **Fewer steps: no.** A's word cer falls at every save (0.51 → 0.40 →
   0.33 at 50 / 100 / 150 a row) while its loss and \|Δ\| are flat after ≈ 75.
4. **The scene is not the cost.** Measured against the EN reference, the
   arms sit at the floor on words and above it on singles (0.96 against
   0.944). A lone glyph that is not drawn large keeps more of the EN scene.
   The loss is in the text alone.
5. **A over B, on the scene and not on the text.** On the text they do not
   separate: words tie at 10, and singles (B 11, A 6) are one render a glyph.
   On the scene, A keeps the scene by eye
   (`results/…-identity-hira_cold_enref/sheets_enref/`: EN ref |
   retrain (0930) | A | B). On p01 (the bedroom) B swaps the girl or the bed
   in places where A keeps them (かなしい 0.834, かんがえ 0.825 against A's
   0.928 / 0.928), and B letters Latin into a word (たすけて → `teetf けて`). By
   the number this is a lean: A's mean en_cos_out is at or above B's in every
   prompt × kind cell (words p00 0.929 / 0.924, p01 0.909 / 0.905, singles
   0.962 / 0.960), but A > B on only 23 / 50 renders. Neither arm draws its
   glyph large or alone in the centre the way the floor does (user, 10-14:
   the encouraging part). A candidate for the next arm keeps A's bands and
   sizes and raises in-word exposure, the dup side (not decided).

## 3. Arm C: the mix toward the windows (`hira_cold_mix`, user 10-14)

`configs/hira_cold_mix.toml` keeps A's rows, bands and 150 steps a row and
moves the mix to grid 20 / lone 5 / bubbleN 55 / bubble1 20, split inside
each form as in A. The data has 8 101 items, 4 455 of them windows (A has
1 620). That is ≈ 1 320 window draws a row against A's ≈ 480, and ≈ 750 in
grids. The window pool is uneven: per glyph min 13, median 4 220. Train job
`20261014-163901-d292a6`, 82 min. Over the last 500 steps in-box / out-box is
0.100 / 0.072, against A's 0.110 / 0.073. The read is the same grid, job

1. **The windows do not bring the words back.** C's hiragana words are at
   1 / 18 exact and 16 / 18 dup, against A's 3 and 14. The hypothesis that
   in-word exposure drives the word dup does not hold. C's repeats move to
   the first glyph: ここんにちは, こことば, ここうえん, かかなきなしい.
2. **Singles improve.** Exact is 10 against A's 6 and dup 12 against 17.
   Both are flat from s8100 (100 a row) on.
3. The scene stays at the floor (word en_cos_out 0.912 against 0.915).

The numbers point to § 1 of `motivation2.md`. The repeats are the base's
text region minus the word, and the slot count commits between σ 0.95 and
0.9 (`inject_count`). None of A's or C's tiers reaches above 0.8, and B's
cap is 0.9. Neither the mix nor the steps act on the σ where the count is
set. This is not tested here.
