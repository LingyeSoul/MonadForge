## Verdict

1. **Both arms lose to the floor.** On words they score 10 / 26 against 19 /
   26, and on singles 6 and 11 against 18.
2. **The identity is mostly there. The failure is repetition.** Singles
   *contained* 17 – 18 against the floor's 19, but *dup* is 12 – 17 against 1:
   あ is drawn ああああ, を as をを, and the words double glyphs (かかなししい,
   かかんががえ, たたいつつ). The grid-heavy mix (≈ 6 glyphs an item) together with
   ¼ of `retrain_kana`'s in-word exposure gives a row that fills cells. This is
   § 8's risk ("words and `dup` decide, not singles"), read as stated. Lone
   glyphs also pick up Latin filler (`xxx.com laone`) more often than on the
   floor.
3. **Fewer steps: no.** A's word cer falls at every save (0.51 → 0.40 →
   0.33 at 50 / 100 / 150 a row) while its loss and \|Δ\| are flat after ≈ 75.
4. **The scene is not the cost.** Measured against the EN reference, the
   arms sit at the floor on words and above it on singles (0.96 against
   0.944). A lone glyph that is not drawn large keeps more of the EN scene.
   The loss is in the text alone.
5. **A over B, on the scene and not on the text.** On the text they do not
   separate: words tie at 10, and singles (B 11, A 6) are one render a glyph.
   On the scene, A keeps the scene by eye
   (`results/…-identity-hira_cold_enref/sheets_enref/`: EN ref |
   retrain (0930) | A | B). On p01 (the bedroom) B swaps the girl or the bed
   in places where A keeps them (かなしい 0.834, かんがえ 0.825 against A's
   0.928 / 0.928), and B letters Latin into a word (たすけて → `teetf けて`). By
   the number this is a lean: A's mean en_cos_out is at or above B's in every
   prompt × kind cell (words p00 0.929 / 0.924, p01 0.909 / 0.905, singles
   0.962 / 0.960), but A > B on only 23 / 50 renders. Neither arm draws its
   glyph large or alone in the centre the way the floor does (user, 10-14:
   the encouraging part). A candidate for the next arm keeps A's bands and
   sizes and raises in-word exposure, the dup side (not decided).

## 3. Arm C: the mix toward the windows (`hira_cold_mix`, user 10-14)

`configs/hira_cold_mix.toml` keeps A's rows, bands and 150 steps a row and
moves the mix to grid 20 / lone 5 / bubbleN 55 / bubble1 20, split inside
each form as in A. The data has 8 101 items, 4 455 of them windows (A has
1 620). That is ≈ 1 320 window draws a row against A's ≈ 480, and ≈ 750 in
grids. The window pool is uneven: per glyph min 13, median 4 220. Train job
`20261014-163901-d292a6`, 82 min. Over the last 500 steps in-box / out-box is
0.100 / 0.072, against A's 0.110 / 0.073. The read is the same grid, job

1. **The windows do not bring the words back.** C's hiragana words are at
   1 / 18 exact and 16 / 18 dup, against A's 3 and 14. The hypothesis that
   in-word exposure drives the word dup does not hold. C's repeats move to
   the first glyph: ここんにちは, こことば, ここうえん, かかなきなしい.
2. **Singles improve.** Exact is 10 against A's 6 and dup 12 against 17.
   Both are flat from s8100 (100 a row) on.
3. The scene stays at the floor (word en_cos_out 0.912 against 0.915).

The numbers point to § 1 of `motivation2.md`. The repeats are the base's
text region minus the word, and the slot count commits between σ 0.95 and
0.9 (`inject_count`). None of A's or C's tiers reaches above 0.8, and B's
cap is 0.9. Neither the mix nor the steps act on the σ where the count is
set. This is not tested here.

## 4. The start (10-15, user): random, stick + ball, stick + predicted glyph

Three arms take A's data, bands, 150 steps a row and seed context, and change
only where the 81 rows start (`init`, `Rows._rand_start`). The norm pull is
centred on the start. All share generator seed 0, so they also share σ / ε
draws (out-box equal at every logged step) and paired in-box is meaningful.

| arm | start | \|row\| at start | job |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hira_cold_rand` | isotropic random direction at the pack row's norm | 190 | `20261015-075337-66929d` |
| `hira_cold_sball` | pack row + 0930's hiragana stick (the 81 rows' mean offset, the 10-03 stick, \|156.0\|) + a random ball ⟂ it at 0930's mean spike (208.7) | 323 | `20261015-095206-cc0bbb` |
| `hira_cold_sglyph` | sball's start with the ball each row's predicted glyph component (`probes/glyph_init.py`, below), centred over the 81, ⟂ the stick | 324 | `20261015-120955-23d149` |

sball and sglyph read the stick off 0930's own hiragana. A new script has
no such stick; the leak-free stick is katakana's (cos 0.913 to hiragana's).

**glyph_init** (CPU, 5 s): each glyph drawn on the em box in the 10
top-level faces (size and position kept), blurred, averaged, PCA 128. A
ridge to `e_i` (`status.md` § 7: offset − family stick, `q̂_i` projected
out) is fitted on 0930's katakana (83) / kanji (1 183) rows and read
against 0930's own 81 hiragana `e_i` (rank of the true row among the 81,
chance 41):

| fitted on | top-1 | mean rank | plain hiragana top-1 / 46 (rank, chance 23.5) | cos |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| katakana | 20 | 10.8 | 12 (9.0) | 0.168 |
| kanji (centred) | 12 | 15.6 | 9 (7.6) | 0.092 |
| katakana + kanji (centred, the init) | 19 | 8.6 | 16 (6.2) | 0.135 |
| shuffled targets | 1 – 3 | 39 – 43 | – | 0.00 |

The 5 bitmap-nearest training glyphs' mean `e` nearly ties the ridge
(katakana: top-1 17, rank 11.9). The misses keep the base glyph and lose the
mark (ほ→ぼ, ぱ→ば, ぺ→べ). This is shape structure in `e_i` that
`cjk_renderable_anima`'s 09-18 read did not see on whole rows ("a shape
neighbour is no better a warm start than μ").

**Training.** In-box loss (last 500 steps) A 0.110, rand 0.114, sball 0.106,
sglyph 0.107. Paired, sball sits under A at every step of warmup (−0.017) and
the gap shrinks to −0.008 after step 2 500. sglyph sits under sball only
early (−0.005 to step 2 000, −0.0002 from 4 000). \|row\|: A and rand settle
at 279 / 287 against 0930's 291.5; sball and sglyph stay ≈ 1.19× (347 / 346).
The random ball lacks 0930's offset lean against its own pack row (cos
−0.19, the `q_i` shrink), and nothing pulls the length back. Against 0930's
rows (CPU): every arm's `e` lands on its own 0930 row among the 81 (A 71,
sball 76, sglyph 78 at s8100) at cos 0.32 – 0.36. sglyph's start (0.135) keeps
its lead: 0.362 against sball's 0.316.

**Read** (`probes/identity.py`, same grid; `results/20261015-0927-…-rand`,
`…-1125-…-sball`, `…-1343-…-sglyph`):

| arm | word exact / 26 | word cer | word dup | hira words exact / 18 | single exact / 24 | single contained | single dup | word / single en_cos_out |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| floor | **19** | **0.113** | 7 | **12** | **18** | 19 | 1 | 0.915 / 0.944 |
| A | 10 | 0.327 | 15 | 3 | 6 | 17 | 17 | 0.919 / 0.962 |
| rand | 7 | 0.510 | 17 | 0 | 5 | 13 | 18 | 0.910 / 0.968 |
| sball | 12 | 0.275 | 14 | 5 | 10 | 19 | 12 | 0.906 / 0.957 |
| sglyph | 9 | 0.356 | 17 | 2 | 13 | 22 | 10 | 0.911 / 0.954 |

Paired on exact against the floor: sball words 3 / 10 (p 0.09), singles 2 /
10 (p 0.04); sglyph words 1 / 11 (p 0.006), singles 2 / 7 (p 0.18). sball
over A 10 / 4, over rand 11 / 1; sglyph against sball words 1 / 4, singles
6 / 3.

1. **The pack row's direction carries more than the glyph.** rand is A one
   save late on word cer (0.510 against A's 0.506 at 50 a row), and its
   singles fall back to Latin: an English-like sentence or a subtitle box on
   ~7 / 24 (も や さ う が り を; several glyphs draw the same sentence
   template), against ~2 for A. め draws a line of めめめ….
2. **The stick brings back the floor's layouts.** sball draws る large and
   centred, り in a black circle, たすけて p01 on the blanket, ことば p01 in
   the floor's composition. No other cold arm does (the § 2 note: "neither
   arm draws its glyph large or centred"). Its singles carry a CJK garble
   before the right glyph instead (が `倒钩示吸がが`, れ, ぜ, や).
3. **The predicted glyph buys singles, not words.** sglyph's singles are the
   best of any cold arm (exact 13, contained 22 over the floor's 19; っ right
   for the first time) and the first not significantly under the floor. Its
   words repeat more (こうえん → `ここううええんん`), and sball's floor
   layouts are mostly gone. One seed; the sball / sglyph gaps are not
   significant either way.
4. **No start touches the word dup.** Word dup is 13 – 17 for every cold arm
   (A, B, C, rand, sball, sglyph) against the floor's 7. The start sets
   identity; the slot count is elsewhere (§ 3, `motivation2.md` § 1).

## 5. The predicted glyph off a PE tower (10-15, user)

`probes/glyph_init.py --features` (job `20261015-141607-22a1eb`, 7 min;
`results/20261015-1423-glyph_init_bitmap+pe_core+pe_core_grid+pe_spatial+pe_spatial_l/`)
swaps § 4's bitmap for a PE tower's features on the same glyphs. Each glyph
is drawn black on white at the tower's native px, the em box scaled from the
bitmap's, and the features are averaged over the faces. Then come the same
PCA 128, ridge and read. The rank is read on predictions centred over the 81,
as the init starts them; this is § 4's read (bitmap kata_kanji 19 / 8.6).

The ceiling is how far independent runs agree. A / B / C (pack start) land
on 0930's hiragana e at cos 0.34 – 0.49, top-1 68 – 78 / 81, rank 1.0 – 1.9.
The cos stays well under 1 because ≈ 60 % of any start's ball survives
training (sball keeps its random ball at cos 0.50, sglyph its predicted ball
at 0.52). Most of `e` is not constrained by the loss, so the read is the rank
and not the cos.

| features (fitted on kata + kanji) | top-1 / 81 | top-5 | rank | cos (centred) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bitmap (§ 4, sglyph's init) | 19 | 49 | 8.6 | 0.134 |
| PE-Core, attn-pooled | 25 | 54 | 6.2 | 0.176 |
| PE-Core, tokens 4×4 | 29 | – | 4.0 | – |
| **PE-Spatial-B16, tokens 4×4** | **45** | **71** | **2.3** | **0.228** |
| PE-Spatial-L14, tokens 4×4 | 40 | 75 | 2.6 | 0.233 |

Shuffled targets give top-1 0 and rank 33 – 36 on every kind. Fitted on kanji
alone, PE-Spatial ranks 5.3, against the bitmap's 15.6, so a script with no
kana-like neighbour still gets a start.

**The (han)dakuten step does not compose.** The mean step e(marked) − e(base)
agrees across the scripts (dakuten: katakana ↔ hiragana cos 0.67), but a marked
row is not its base plus the step (cos(e(は), e(ば)) 0.25; a pair's step is
larger than \|e\|). Adding the katakana step to the 26 marked predictions
raises cos and costs identity on every kind (PE-Spatial top-1 45 → 27).
Not used.
