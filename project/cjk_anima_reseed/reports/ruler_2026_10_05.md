# The dialogue ruler: built, its floor, the first arms (2026-10-05)

From `criteria.md` (10-04): the banner grid's verdicts are the banner's; an
arm is judged on JA dialogue in a speech bubble, read per render against a
floor rendered once. The user set the sources (10-05): the training set's
own bubble dialogue and its captions.

## 1. The ruler

`ruler.py build` → `output/cjk_anima_reseed/ruler/ruler.json` (the dataset's
text stays out of the repo; `ruler.tsv` to read by eye).

- **Strings**: the `Japanese text reads as` clause (not SFX) of the 136
  captions tagged `speech bubble` / `comic` / `dialogue`; one string per
  image, 32 a bin — short 2–4 glyphs (16 interjections, 16 lexical), mid
  5–9, long 10–20. Dropped: Latin, digits, a kanji the dataset has once, a
  core already drawn (`はぁっ…` = `はぁ`), and 31 strings by eye (`DROP`: OCR
  misreads — `出未` for 出来, `ロゾけ` for 口づけ —, SFX filed as dialogue,
  fragments).
- **Prompts**: each string's own image caption, the text clauses and the
  other-language text tags out, `speech bubble, japanese text` in; rating,
  characters and artist as captioned (all 136 are explicit / nsfw /
  sensitive).
- **EN reference**: each line's EN rendering (hand-written, `en.json`),
  same prompt with `english text`, `English text reads as "…"`.
- **Render**: the source image's aspect (1:2 – 2:1) at 768² pixels (13
  shapes, 672×896 the most common), seed 0, 28 steps, cfg 4, routed; one
  `ExtDelta` over the union of the arms' ext ids (2683), each arm's table
  swapped in (a row an arm lacks = the pack row). Plumbing: retrain_kana's
  cached plain render `p00 こんにちは` re-rendered on this path, mean |Δpx|
  4.02.
- **Read**: both readers on every detector box and the whole page, the boxes
  also joined in column order (right to left) and line order — a bubble in
  two columns is one string. Text: `official` (both readers exact),
  `exact` (either), `contained`, ≤ 1 / ≤ 2 edits (strings over 2 glyphs),
  `cer` (the best read's edits / length, capped 1), `dup` (the best read has
  a doubled glyph the string lacks, or is longer than it). Page: EN-ref cos
  pooled (`en_cos`) and token-wise outside every text box of both images
  (`en_tok_out`, PE-Spatial on the encoder's bucket grid — Axis 2's ruler),
  flat-white share over the EN ref's (`fw_over_en`).
- **Seen / unseen**: `cov3` = the string's trigrams' share in the arms of
  record's training text (kana_up, ball_rk_bubble, retrain_kana data);
  unseen = `cov3 ≤ 0.15`: 41 strings, 27 of them short.

**Noise.** Within a process the same rows re-render to the same pixels
(50 floor pairs); across processes they do not: ball_rk_bubble differs from retrain_kana only in its
81 hiragana rows, and its 6 hiragana-free strings render 1.6–6.7 mean |Δpx|
apart. The reads hold: all 6 give the same exact / cer, en_tok_out within
0.01. A per-render en_tok_out difference under ~0.01 is noise; the text
pairs are not.

## 5. The punct fix: marks retrained in words (10-05)

`～ … ♡ ♥ 、 。` cold, every other row frozen at seed_retrain_0930.

- **Pack**: `punct_pack.py` → `anima_cjk_vocab_pack_punct` (the raw pack +
  `fold` / `dots` + row 69 558 at T5's `...`); `ext_vocab.Dots`,
  `tests/test_ext_vocab_dots.py`.
- **Data** (`configs/punct.toml`, 600 items, 105–120 per row): mark windows

  dialogue lines with ellipses normalised, the training set's text and 3 983
