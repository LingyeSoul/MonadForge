# The micro arms A / B / B′ — 2026-10-11

User 10-11: the three warm modes for KO dialogue (`proposal_jamo` § 4) read
cheaply before the full build. One data build, three arms, one read.

## Setup

- **Rows**: the 192 most frequent chatbot syllables (`corpus.py --rows 192`);
  the lines whose every syllable is among them (`ko_chat_192`: 3 153 of
  15 520; 2 614 pass the sent filter). Every arm sees the same data
  (`ko_micro_a`'s build, 19 200 items: bubble1 5 %, bubbleN 25 %, sentko
  70 %; KO lines horizontal, 70 % left-aligned; windows whole words).
- **Recipe**: warm from `cjk_anima_reseed/jamo_j256_ko`, 20 steps a row
  (3 840 steps, batch 4), lr 2e-4 cosine, free_residual 0, no L_pres, punct
  pack, seed_1008's JA rows frozen. ~27 min each on the local GPU (2.3 it/s).
- **Arms**: A = the 192 rows free (`ko_micro_a`); B = the 192 composed from
  the jamo factors only, the factors at j256_ko's (`ko_micro_b`,
  `factor_from`); B′ = B + a per-syllable residual, no pull on it
  (`ko_micro_bp`). B's step-1 loss equals A's (0.1243): the factors at
  j256_ko's compose its rows exactly, so the three start at one point.
- **Read** (`probes/micro_read.py`; results `results/20261011-2324-micro-read/`):
  each item alone in a KO bubble, seed 0, under floor (= jamo_j256_ko) and
  the three arms; v4 free read. Groups (`assets/micro192.json`): `trained`
  (every third of the 192, 64), `next` (ranks 193–256, in no line: 64),
  `held` (the ruler's strings on the 192: 27, mean 6.1 syllables).

## 2. Every arm learned "a KO bubble holds a line"

A single syllable in a bubble draws a line under every arm (sheets
`sheet_trained_*`: 사 → 세살사은개마. / 세산 슬자마. / 사 상뒤따; 요 →
엘지 온카새돔요): drawn 3.1 → 5.5–6.2 on `trained`, 2.0 → 7.4–10.3 on
`held` (targets 6.1). The lone syllable's identity falls with it: exact
18 → 1–2 of 64, jamo F1 0.43 → 0.18–0.24. The data is 70 % sentko lines and
5 % lone syllables (bubble1); 20 steps a row put the line's length and
layout into the rows. The JA warm passes lost short strings too
(`../cjk_anima_reseed/status.md` § 2: short kana exact 8 → 3); this is the
same loss at full strength.

## 3. The modes, under that

- **B / B′ carry it to the untrained syllables.** `next` exact 8 → 2, drawn
  3.4 → 5.4–6.1: the factors spread the line habit to every syllable, A
  leaves them at the floor. With this mix, sharing is a cost.
- **On the held strings B′ leads**: glyph F1 0.27 (B 0.21, A 0.18, floor
  0.17), jamo F1 over chance 0.19 (B 0.15, floor 0.15, A 0.13). Every arm
  draws ~1.6× the target's letters there, so chance rises with them (0.20 →
  0.30–0.33); the lead is small against that.
- No arm reads a held string exactly (floor 1 / 27).

## 4. Not separated

- The data mix and the mode: all three share the failure in § 2, so the
  read cannot say which mode keeps lone identity under a mix that does not
  teach the line habit.
- One render per item, seed 0.
