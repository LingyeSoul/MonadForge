## What it does not separate

One seed, one render per item for the first read; the hits swap both ways
(14 floor-only vs 20 cf-only across the 149); four seeds on H / next keep
the direction (above). The arm changes three things at once — the
counterfactual forms, a 25 % flat-canvas grid tier at σ 0.3–0.75, and 13 %
less of the built mix — so the read cannot say the forms (rather than more
low-σ grid items in Noto Serif) carried H. Arms that would: the same tier
with the hosts drawn as their own full syllables (cf off, tier kept). g44
does not stand in for it: it cut the 16 px tiers (σ 0.2–0.6) as it grew
grid_44, where cf adds σ 0.3–0.75 and keeps them.
