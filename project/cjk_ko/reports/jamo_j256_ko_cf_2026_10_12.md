## What it does not separate

One seed, one render per item; the hits swap both ways (14 floor-only vs 20
cf-only across the 149). The arm changes three things at once — the
counterfactual forms, a 25 % flat-canvas grid tier at σ 0.3–0.75, and 13 %
less of the built mix — so the read cannot say the forms (rather than more
low-σ grid items in Noto Serif) carried H. Arms that would: the same tier
with the hosts drawn as their own full syllables (cf off, tier kept), and
more seeds on H / next before reading 10 vs 8 as a step.
