## What it does not separate

The table couples size and σ: the 16 px tiers train at σ 0.2–0.6, grid_44 at
0.55–0.75, so cutting them also cuts the low-σ share, where a jamo's strokes
are told apart. The read cannot say whether the small glyphs or their σ band
carried what was lost. Arms that would: a larger tier with its band widened
down (grid_44 / the opt-in `grid_64` at σ from 0.2), or the 16 px share moved
to the 29 px tiers.
